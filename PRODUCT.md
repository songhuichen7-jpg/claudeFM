# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is veko, using Claudio FM as a private single-listener radio. The user is listening, chatting with a DJ persona, asking for songs by mood or prompt, and saving taste signals while the app stays ready like a small desktop device.

## Product Purpose

Claudio FM is a private AI radio: a music player plus a speaking DJ that understands taste, recommends tracks, and turns listening into a conversational loop. Success means the real `src/` app visually and behaviorally matches the approved prototype and reference video while keeping the live backend, playback, liked library, focus view, settings, and theme workflows intact.

## Brand Personality

Terminal, editorial, restrained. Claudio should feel like a tasteful engineer's always-on radio: precise, intimate, slightly opinionated, and never like a generic AI music dashboard.

## Anti-references

Do not use a Spotify-style two-column layout, mood-picker dashboards, vinyl cover rotation, purple gradients, large decorative glow, black-disc metaphors, or generic AI app decoration. Do not introduce a new visual language outside the approved `prototype/` system.

## Design Principles

Keep the device frame central: one focused column, hairline borders, dense but breathable information.

Let the single accent color carry state only: live dots, active highlights, transport state, liked state, focus, and progress.

Prefer terminal clarity over decoration: monospace UI, restrained surfaces, precise labels, and no ornamental motion.

Make the DJ feel alive through state and timing: live pulses, EQ motion, word highlighting, waveform movement, and responsive feedback.

Protect the approved prototype: when `src/` diverges from `prototype/`, the prototype is the visual and interaction source of truth unless a newer reference video supersedes it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced motion and keep animated feedback functional rather than decorative. Maintain readable contrast in both dark and light modes, preserve keyboard-operable controls, and keep dense UI labels legible on mobile and desktop.
